# Imagery: generated stills and B-roll

One photographic look for everything a model paints. The style block below is appended, verbatim, after the beat's own content in every still prompt; it describes how a picture is rendered and never what is in it, so it cannot contradict a beat. Subject, setting, people, objects and framing are the beat's to decide. Animation and graphics are HyperFrames, never generated.

## stills.style_block

> Warm morning daylight from a shop window, soft and directional, with gentle contrast and open shadows. Natural palette of cream, light oak, green plants and worn brick, shadows falling to deep green rather than black; the only saturated colour is a touch of warm amber. Eye level on a 35 to 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, room to breathe around the subject. Modern digital photograph, lightly desaturated, fine grain, tidy lived-in small businesses, relaxed unposed expressions.

Phrased positively throughout because the current models take no negative prompt. Reference images are optional; when a set of eight to fifteen approved stills exists, derive the block from them and replace this one.

## A menu for whoever writes the beat

Not appended. Terms the models understand, to choose from when a beat needs them:

- **Framing**: wide establishing, medium, close-up, extreme close-up on hands or paperwork, over-the-shoulder, two-shot.
- **Angle**: eye level by default; high angle for a counter or desk overview.
- **Light**: window side light, overcast daylight, a single practical such as a pendant lamp.
- **Camera, video pass only**: locked-off, slow push-in, slow lateral slide, tracking alongside, handheld drift. One move per clip, already in motion at the first frame.
- **Duration**: four to eight seconds.

## Composited, never generated

The mark, screens and any legible text, per the house rule. They are placed from `assets/Logos` and the HyperFrames compositions after the image exists.
