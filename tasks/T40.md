# T40 suggestions appear as editable cards in the Visuals rail
Why (Travis, first real use 2026-10-13): after "Suggest visuals" the only trace is dashed underlines plus hover
Accept/Adjust/Dismiss, and the Visuals rail stays empty ("Your words stay on the left…"). He expected a card per
proposed visual on the right, editable there. Hard to see what is what.
Read first: `web/src/suggest/{SuggestOverlay,useSuggestions,api}.ts(x)`, the Visuals rail + card components
(`web/src/enrich/`), `edit/transcript/RawTranscript.tsx`, `post_api/routes/suggest.py`, T13b and T30c specs.
Build:
1. Every pending suggestion renders as a **proposed card** in the Visuals rail, in transcript order, merged with
   accepted cards. It is visually distinct: dashed border, "Proposed" label, and a medium chip (live action /
   motion graphic / overlay …). It shows the quoted words it covers, the brief, the direction note, the rationale
   (collapsed) and flags.
2. Edit on the card before accepting: the brief and direction note are editable inline, and the medium is picked
   from a select. Edits persist on the suggestion (add a PATCH endpoint plus a test). **Accept** turns it into the
   normal card in place, carrying the edits. **Dismiss** removes it. "Accept all" stays.
3. Two-way highlight: hovering or clicking a card highlights its words (the dashed passage) and scrolls them into
   view; clicking a dashed passage focuses and scrolls to its card. Each card and its passage share a small number
   badge (1, 2, 3…) so they read as a pair. Keep the hover toolbar on the words too.
4. While suggesting: the button shows a spinner and "Suggesting visuals… (~20 s)", and the rail shows a placeholder.
   On failure the error appears in the rail as well as by the button.
5. Fix the overlap: the Suggest / Accept all controls must not cover the "Read the story…" hint. Put them in the rail
   header instead.
Acceptance: vitest for the card states (proposed, edit, accept carries edits, dismiss) and the badge pairing; API test
for PATCH; Playwright (mocked API): suggest → N proposed cards in the rail → edit brief → accept → normal card shows
the edited brief; click passage ↔ card focus. tsc clean, all suites green.
Out of scope: generation changes, new mediums.
