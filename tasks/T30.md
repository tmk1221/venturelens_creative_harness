## Taxonomy (source: the current app, `~/cc/post/backend/app/routers/story_board.py:47-55` and `~/cc/vl/scripts/acme-automations/beats.py`; improve, don't copy)
A beat has a PICTURE layer and an optional OVERLAY layer; the two are independent and an overlay may sit over any picture, including generated live action.
- Picture: `aroll` (presenter, nothing over it) · `provided` (my footage: a clip + in/out) · `live_action` (generated: brief → start frames → pick → motion → video, optional end frame) · `motion_graphic` (HyperFrames, full frame, opaque).
- Overlay (HyperFrames, alpha): `depth` = in_front | behind (behind ⇒ measure + cutout nodes; only meaningful over aroll) · `position` = over aroll: right | left | lower-band (in front) or right | left | full-frame (behind); over any B-roll: right | left | lower-band | center | full-width. Keep the old swap rule (lower-band ⇄ full-frame when depth changes). Each overlay has its own brief and its own recipe/graph.
- Every beat: brief (what the viewer sees; provenance human|agent), direction note, flags (must-have shot, presenter stays on camera, picture-must-match-words), density hint, recurring cast references, model per generate node.

- Recurring cast: a `character` node that references the project cast registry (T12) and feeds still.generate continuity.
