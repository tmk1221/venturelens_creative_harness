# T08 edit operations
Goal: the transcript editor's brain, as pure functions over a Cut (UI comes later). Plan §5, §3.1 borrowed patterns.
Read first: T02 model + store/undo.
Build `packages/post_core/post_core/edit/` (stdlib + pydantic):
- `ops.py`: pure `Cut -> Cut` operations, all addressed by word ids: `cut_from_transcript` (one passage over everything), `delete_words(range)` (splits a passage), `restore_words`, `move_passage` / `move_words(range, before_word)`, `insert_range(transcript_id, word range, at)` (pull a take from anywhere in any sync group — takes are just other word ranges), `replace_range_with(range, other range)` (swap a take), `set_angle(range, source_id)` (splits passages as needed), `set_trim(passage, head, tail)`, `derive_cut(parent, passages subset, aspect)` for shorts, `merge_adjacent` normalisation after every op (adjacent passages contiguous in the same transcript with same angle merge).
- Span behaviour: spans anchor to words; deleting words under a span shrinks it, deleting all its words marks it `orphaned` (kept, reported), moving a passage carries its spans. Every op returns `(cut, EditReport{affected_spans, orphaned_spans})`.
- `ranges.py`: passages → keep ranges in source seconds per sync group with `merge_gap` (default 0.35 s) and default handles; `timeline(cut)` → ordered segments with derived timeline start/duration (rational frames at the cut's fps) — the ONLY place timeline time is computed; `word_at(timeline_time)` and `time_of(word)` mappings for playback-follow.
- `heuristics.py`: filler words and long-silence detection returning word ranges (suggestions, never auto-applied).
- `session.py`: apply op → validate invariants → store.write with revision → push undo snapshot; gesture coalescing (begin/end gesture = one undo step).
Acceptance: pytest property-style checks: any op sequence keeps invariants; delete+restore is identity; move carries spans; angle change only re-splits; derive_cut shares word ids with parent; timeline durations sum correctly at 23.976 and 29.97; undo/redo through session; orphan reporting.
Only touch `post_core/edit/` and `tests/edit/`. Do not edit pyproject.toml.
